Local polish works immediately. For contextual rewrites, configure an OpenAI-compatible provider on the server and restart it:

WRITING_API_KEY=your-key
WRITING_API_BASE=https://api.openai.com/v1
WRITING_MODEL=gpt-4.1-mini

Keep keys out of source control. Connected mode sends your text to your chosen provider, which may charge for usage. The app itself has no request quotas.
